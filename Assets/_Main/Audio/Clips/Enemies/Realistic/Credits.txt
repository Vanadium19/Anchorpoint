Real recordings, all released under CC0 1.0 (public domain); attribution is not required.
Clips were trimmed, faded, normalized and converted to mono 16-bit.

Explosions (grenade_explosion_*, drone_explosion_*)
  Source: USC Sound Effect Archive, SSE Library EXPLOSIONS
  https://archive.org/details/SSE_Library_EXPLOSIONS
  Files: Grenade explodes; Explosion bomb or shell; Land explosion; Dull explosion; Explosion good; Heavy sharp explosion with reverb.

Drone rotor loop (drone_rotor_loop)
  Source: The Designer's Choice Collection, AIRCRAFT / Radio Controlled
  https://archive.org/details/Designers-Choice-Collection-Aircraft
  File: Samsung Galaxy Smartphone CU Drone Flying (the middle of the take, looped with a crossfade).

Countdown beep (drone_beep)
  Source: USC Sound Effect Archive, SSE Library BEEPS
  https://archive.org/details/SSE_Library_BEEPS
  File: Cardiac arrest alarm (1 kHz tone), one short beep.

Whooshes (whoosh_*)
  Source: USC Sound Effect Archive, SSE Library WEAPONS
  https://archive.org/details/SSE_Library_WEAPONS
  File: Whip whoosh, individual swishes.
